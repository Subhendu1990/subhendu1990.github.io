About Me
A decade of shipping software that has to work on Monday morning — from the database schema to the last pixel of the UI.
Hi There! I'm Subhendu Patra

I'm a full stack engineer with over 10 years of experience designing, building and running web applications end to end. I've worked across the whole stack — modelling data and writing APIs in Node.js, NestJS and Java, then building the interfaces on top of them in React, Next.js and Angular.

Most of my work has been on long-lived products rather than throwaway builds: replacing legacy modules without downtime, splitting monoliths into services, cutting page loads from seconds to milliseconds, and setting up the CI/CD and monitoring that keeps a release boring. I care about code that the next engineer can read, and I'm just as comfortable in a code review or an architecture discussion as I am in an IDE.

These days I take on selected freelance and consulting engagements alongside my full-time work — usually greenfield builds, architecture reviews, or helping a team get an overdue release out the door.
